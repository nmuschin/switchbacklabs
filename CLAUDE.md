## Working preferences

These apply across all my repos; the full version (the studio, every repo, environment gotchas) is in `CLAUDE.md` in nmuschin/codeai. Anything specific to this repo, below or in `AGENTS.md`, wins.

- **Suggestions before changes.** Before changing something that already exists, especially a logo, an icon or a design, lay out the options with a recommendation and wait for my pick.
- **Ask clarifying questions** when a request is ambiguous; keep explanations clear and short.
- **Branch and PR for every change**, saying what changed and how it was checked. I usually ask for the merge myself (squash).
- **Say what you couldn't verify**, and tell me how to check it myself.
- **The studio-wide to-do list** is `TODO.md` in nmuschin/switchbacklabs. When work here lands, tick it off there.
