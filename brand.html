Using the marks

  • Leave room. Keep clear space around a logo of at least a quarter of the tile’s width.
  • Tile first. Use the tile wherever you can. Use the bare mark only where a tile won’t fit.
  • Small sizes. At 32 px and below, use the favicon. Its bolder trail stays readable where the stops would blur.
  • Don’t edit. Keep the colours, proportions and wordmark as they are. Don’t recolour the stops individually.

The wordmarks are set in Plus Jakarta Sans and outlined, so no font is needed. Plus Jakarta Sans is licensed under the SIL Open Font License. Every file is generated by tools/build.sh.
