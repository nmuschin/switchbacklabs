Bellwether Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Bellwether keeps your bell schedule on your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch, and nothing else. It makes no network requests. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores

Your class periods, their bell times and weekdays, and your reminder settings.

iPhone and Apple Watch

Your schedule is copied between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch directly, using Apple’s Watch Connectivity. It is not sent to a server.

Notifications

The haptic taps before each bell are time-sensitive notifications scheduled on your devices. The watch complication reads the schedule from your watch.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting services, advertising or tracking.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting your data

Delete periods in the app, or delete Bellwether from your iPhone and Apple Watch to remove everything.

Children

Bellwether is used by students and teachers. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
