CellarStories Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

CellarStories has two parts. The iPhone and Mac app is a private journal: your notes stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. The website, cellarstories.app, is where you join wine clubs and share notes, so it needs an account and stores what you choose to share there.

The app

Your wines, tasting notes, ratings and photos are stored on your device and synced through your private iCloud database, handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Nothing goes to the website unless you export a journal file and import it there yourself. The app has no analytics, ads or tracking.

The website: what we store

  • Account: your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time link.
  • Profile: your display name, and an optional photo and bio.
  • Clubs: the clubs you create or join, and invitations. An emailed invitation stores the invitee’s email address so the invite can be sent.
  • Notes you add: wines, tastings, ratings, tasting notes and any photos, including photos of journal pages.

Who can see it

A note is visible to you and to the people it’s shared with: members of your club or the people at the same tasting. Photos are kept in private storage and shown only to people allowed to see the note. Nothing is public, and nothing is shown to people outside your clubs.

Service providers

The website runs on Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage, in the United States) and Resend (sign-in and invitation emails). They process data only to run the service.

Cookies

The website uses a cookie only to keep you signed in. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

What we don’t do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, in the app or on the website.
  • No data is sold, or shared beyond the providers above.

Deleting your data

On the website, delete your account in Settings → Delete account. That removes your profile, your notes and their photos. In the app, delete entries, or delete the app; to remove the synced copy, delete CellarStories’ data in your device’s iCloud settings.

Age

CellarStories is about wine and is intended for adults of legal drinking age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
