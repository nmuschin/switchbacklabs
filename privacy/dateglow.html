DateGlow Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

DateGlow keeps your countdowns on your device and syncs them through your own private iCloud account. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and Switchback Labs never receives your data.

What the app stores

The events you create (names, dates, how they repeat, their colours and reminder settings) and your preferences.

iCloud sync

When iCloud is available on your device, your events are stored in your private iCloud database so they appear on your other Apple devices. This is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Switchback Labs cannot see or access it.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Widgets and the menu bar read your events from storage shared only between the app and its widgets on your device.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting services, advertising or tracking.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting your data

Delete events in the app, or delete the app to remove what’s stored on the device. To remove the synced copy, delete DateGlow’s data in your device’s iCloud settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
