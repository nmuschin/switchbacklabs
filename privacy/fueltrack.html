FuelTrack Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

FuelTrack keeps your vehicles and fill-ups on your device and syncs them through your own private iCloud account. Receipts are read on the device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and Switchback Labs never receives your data.

What the app stores

Your vehicles (nickname, make, model, year, VIN and fuel type) and each fill-up: station, location, fuel type, gallons, prices, odometer, trip details, notes and any receipt photo you attach.

iCloud sync

This data is stored in your private iCloud database so it appears on your other Apple devices. It is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Switchback Labs cannot see or access it.

Permissions

  • Location (while using the app): records where you filled up and finds nearby gas stations. The station search uses Apple Maps, so the search and your approximate location go to Apple to return results, under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Camera and Photos: let you scan or import a receipt. Only the images you choose are used.

Receipt reading

Text on a receipt is recognised on your device. Where Apple Intelligence is available, its on-device model helps read the fields. Receipts are not uploaded to us or to any other service.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting services, advertising or tracking.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting your data

Delete entries or vehicles in the app, or delete the app to remove what’s stored on the device. To remove the synced copy, delete FuelTrack’s data in your device’s iCloud settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
