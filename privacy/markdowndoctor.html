MarkdownDoctor Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

MarkdownDoctor works on the files you open on your Mac. It makes no network requests: the preview, maths, diagrams and code highlighting all run from libraries inside the app. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What the app accesses

Only the documents you open or save, and the folders you grant access to so images in a document can be shown. The app is sandboxed, so macOS limits it to those files.

What the app stores

Your preferences (themes, fonts, editor settings) and the list of recent documents, on your Mac.

A note on the network permission

The app includes macOS’s “outgoing connections” permission because the system’s web view needs it to draw the preview at all. MarkdownDoctor itself never connects to anything.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting services, advertising or tracking.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting your data

Your documents are yours and stay where you saved them. Deleting the app removes its preferences.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
