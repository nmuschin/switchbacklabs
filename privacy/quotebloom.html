QuoteBloom Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

QuoteBloom keeps your quotes on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they also sync through your own private iCloud account. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and Switchback Labs never receives your data.

What the app stores

The quotes you add or import, their authors, categories and favourites, and your settings. All of it is stored on your device.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, your quotes are stored in your private iCloud database so they appear on your other Apple devices. That data is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Switchback Labs cannot see or access it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, QuoteBloom schedules a daily quote reminder on your device. Nothing is sent to a server to do this.

Widgets and sharing

The Home Screen widget reads your quotes from storage shared only between the app and its widget on your device. When you share a quote card, it goes only where you choose to send it.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting services, advertising or tracking.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting your data

Delete quotes in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on the device. If you used iCloud sync, you can also remove QuoteBloom’s data in your device’s iCloud settings.

Children

QuoteBloom does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
