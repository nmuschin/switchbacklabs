Support

Something not working, or an idea for one of the apps? Email CONTACT_EMAIL. Switchback Labs is one person, so replies usually take a day or two.

What helps

  • The app’s name and version (in Settings or the About window).
  • Your device and its iOS, iPadOS, watchOS or macOS version.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps a lot.

Common questions

My data isn’t showing up on my other device

Apps that sync (QuoteBloom, FuelTrack, DateGlow and the CellarStories app) use your own iCloud account. Make sure you’re signed in to the same Apple Account on both devices and that iCloud is turned on for the app in Settings → your name → iCloud. QuoteBloom’s sync is off until you turn it on in its Settings. The first sync can take a few minutes.

I’m not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for the app in Settings → Notifications. For Bellwether, also allow time-sensitive notifications so the taps come through Focus modes.

How do I delete my data?

Each app’s privacy policy explains what it stores and how to delete it. For a CellarStories account, use Settings → Delete account on cellarstories.app.
